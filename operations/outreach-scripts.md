# Nexura Labs outreach scripts

Use these as a starting point. Personalise the opening sentence with one genuine observation about the business. Do not claim an existing relationship or guaranteed result.

## First message

Hi [Name], I came across [Business Name] and noticed [specific genuine observation]. I design professional websites for local businesses, and I think a clearer mobile website could help customers understand your services and contact you more easily.

I can prepare a short, no-obligation homepage concept for you to review. Would you be open to seeing it?

— Afiq, Nexura Labs

## Demo follow-up

Hi [Name], I prepared the concept I mentioned for [Business Name]: [demo URL]

It is an independent preview, not a live replacement for your current online presence. I used public information only, and every detail can be changed. If the direction feels useful, I can explain the scope, timeline, and exact price before any work begins.

## Follow-up after two or three days

Hi [Name], just checking whether you had a chance to view the concept. No pressure at all—if improving the website is not a priority right now, I am happy to close the loop.

## If they are interested

Thanks for taking a look. The best next step is a short call or WhatsApp chat about your main goal, required pages, content, and any booking or payment needs. After that, I will send a clear scope and quotation. Full production begins after the agreed deposit.

## Outreach rules

- Contact only relevant businesses and keep the volume reasonable.
- Personalise every message; avoid bulk-spam wording.
- Label speculative demos as independent concepts.
- Use only public, licensed, or client-approved content.
- Never invent reviews, results, awards, partnerships, or client status.
- Stop following up when someone declines or asks not to be contacted.
