Common questions

Useful answers before we talk.

Still unsure which package fits? Start with your business goal, not a feature list.

How long does a website take?

The timeline depends on the number of pages, content readiness and functionality. A realistic delivery window is confirmed with the project scope before work begins.

Do I need to provide photos and content?

For the final website, you should provide accurate business information and approved assets such as your logo, shop, team, service and product photos. I can help organise the content and identify what is missing.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

Starter includes first-year hosting. Business includes a domain and hosting for the first year. Renewals and ongoing care are made clear before approval.

Can you integrate WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp buttons and prepared enquiry flows can make it easier for customers to contact the business with useful information.

Can you add booking or payment functionality?

Yes, where appropriate. These features are scoped based on the required provider, workflow and complexity, then quoted before development.

Can you manage the website after launch?

Yes. Optional monthly or annual website care is available. Larger redesigns, new pages and new functionality are quoted separately.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit is required before full production begins. The remaining 50% is paid before final launch or handover. Complex projects may use agreed milestones.

Can I request changes?

Starter includes one revision and Business includes two. A revision means a collected round of reasonable changes within the agreed scope. New scope is quoted separately.
