# Nexura website instructions

These instructions apply to everything under `apps/nexura-website/`.

- Treat this application as the public Nexura Labs agency website and keep its brand, offer, lead-generation copy, styles, scripts, configuration, and assets within this directory.
- Do not add Maymori-specific copy, assets, styling, configuration, or contact details.
- Keep pricing, payment terms, service claims, contact destinations, canonical URLs, and structured metadata accurate and consistent with the approved Nexura offer.
- Do not claim clients, reviews, results, awards, partnerships, or performance that cannot be substantiated.
- The project-brief form currently creates and copies a brief rather than submitting it; do not imply successful delivery unless a real destination is implemented and tested.
- Keep shared imports client-neutral and source them only from `packages/shared-components/`.
- When changing the public domain or repository URL, review the canonical and social metadata in this app's `index.html` together with the root `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`.
